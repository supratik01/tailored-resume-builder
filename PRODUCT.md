# Tailored Resume Builder — Product Context

## Users
Mid-career professionals (3–12 years exp), primarily software engineers, product managers, and designers. They apply to 3–8 roles at a time. They know their resume is good but know it isn't tailored. They're intelligent, time-pressed, and mildly skeptical of AI tools that overpromise.

## Product Purpose
Upload a resume (PDF/DOCX) + paste a job description. AI rewrites bullets to surface relevant experience, scores the output against ATS keyword requirements, and exports a clean PDF/DOCX ready to submit. No fabrication. Precision over magic.

## Brand Voice
Precise. Direct. No hype. The product does work — let that show. Verbs over adjectives. Show don't tell.

## Anti-references
- LinkedIn: corporate blue, badge/credential overload
- Zety/Resume.com: loud "templates", fake score gamification
- Generic AI SaaS: purple gradients, "powered by AI" everywhere
- Canva: decorative, anti-ATS by design

## Strategic Principles
1. Truthful by default — reframing, never fabricating
2. Speed — results in under 60 seconds
3. ATS-first — clean output, not beautiful output
4. Professional trust — feels like a serious tool, not a game
